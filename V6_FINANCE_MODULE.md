## Important accounting treatment

The Unified report has three separate concepts that must not be mixed:

1. **Operating revenue and expenses** — orders, refunds, selling fees, FBA fees, advertising, storage, promotions, adjustments and liquidation activity.
2. **Tax pass-through** — taxes collected from customers and marketplace-withheld tax offset each other in this report.
3. **Settlement cash flow** — `Transfer` means Amazon paid funds to the bank. It reduces the Amazon ledger but is not an expense.

The Finance page therefore reports both **Operating Net Proceeds** and **Ledger Movement After Transfers**.
